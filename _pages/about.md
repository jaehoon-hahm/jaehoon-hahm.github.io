Hi! I am a Physics Ph.D. candidate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by [Bryan K. Clark](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yIeKkzIAAAAJ&hl=en). With Bryan, I study quantum many-body systems using neural quantum states, quantum Monte Carlo, and generative methods for learning quantum states from classical shadows. I also collaborate with [Romit R. Choudhury](https://croy.web.engr.illinois.edu/) on compositional alignment and reward steering for diffusion models, and with [Jaewoong Choi](https://sites.google.com/view/jaewoongchoi/) on reward steering of one-step generators. Previously, I earned an M.S. in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by [Joonseok Lee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=M-MfqpMAAAAJ&hl=en), and a B.S. in Physics with a minor in Mathematics there.

**Research interests**

- Geometric deep learning and generative modeling for AI for science.
- Optimal transport and geometry-aware diffusion and flow models.
- Discrete flow matching for materials and molecular structures.
- Neural quantum states, quantum Monte Carlo, and subspace diagonalization.

At [Meta Reality Labs](https://about.meta.com/realitylabs/), I am a Research Scientist Intern working with [Jogendra N. Kundu](https://sites.google.com/view/jogendra/) on **self-distillation, self-supervised learning, and representation learning for AR/VR devices**, including eye tracking with limited target-device data.
