About

Jaehoon Hahm

Physics Ph.D. candidate · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I work at the intersection of AI for science, generative modeling, and quantum many-body physics. My research includes geometric deep learning, flow matching, and neural quantum states.

At UIUC, I am advised by Bryan K. Clark. Previously, I earned an M.S. in Data Science and a B.S. in Physics and Mathematics from Seoul National University. I am also a Research Scientist Intern at Meta Reality Labs, working on eye tracking foundation models.
